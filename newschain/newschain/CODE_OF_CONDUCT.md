

# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **NewsChain** community a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering an open, collaborative, and respectful environment where everyone can contribute, learn, and grow.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
* Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
* Helping new contributors get started.
* Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
* Focusing on what is best for the community.
* Showing empathy toward other community members.
* Collaborating in good faith.
* Encouraging learning and knowledge sharing.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, bullying, intimidation, or discrimination.
* Personal attacks or insulting comments.
* Hate speech or abusive language.
* Trolling or deliberately provoking conflicts.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Threats of violence or intimidation.
* Sexual language or unwelcome advances.
* Spam, malicious links, or phishing attempts.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions or development.
* Knowingly submitting malicious code, malware, or exploits intended to harm the project or its users.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including but not limited to:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Documentation
* Project Wiki
* Community chat platforms
* Social media interactions representing the project
* Conferences, meetups, or events related to NewsChain

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the NewsChain project in public spaces.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

When reporting an incident, include as much information as possible, such as:

* Description of the incident
* Date and time
* Links to relevant discussions or content
* Any supporting evidence

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

---

# Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
* Reviewing reported incidents fairly.
* Taking appropriate and proportionate action.
* Protecting the privacy and safety of those involved.
* Maintaining a welcoming environment for all contributors.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

# Enforcement Guidelines

Community impacts will be evaluated using the following guidelines.

## 1. Correction

**Community Impact**

Use of inappropriate language or other behavior that is considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**

A private, written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate and how it can be improved.

---

## 2. Warning

**Community Impact**

A single incident or repeated inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**

A formal warning with clear expectations for future conduct. Continued violations may result in temporary restrictions.

---

## 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**

A serious violation or repeated misconduct.

**Consequence**

A temporary ban from interacting with the project community for a period determined by the maintainers.

---

## 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**

Demonstrated pattern of harmful behavior, harassment, discrimination, threats, or malicious actions.

**Consequence**

A permanent ban from participating in the NewsChain community.

---

# Contributions

By contributing to NewsChain, you agree to:

* Follow this Code of Conduct.
* Respect maintainers and contributors.
* Participate in discussions professionally.
* Accept code review feedback respectfully.
* Help maintain a positive community.

---

# Security

If you discover a security vulnerability, **do not open a public GitHub Issue**.

Instead, please follow the instructions in **SECURITY.md** to report it responsibly.
